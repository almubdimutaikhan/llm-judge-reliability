# Results

Generated by `python scripts/analyze.py` from the six released data files.

## Generator-family diagnostic

On matched TRIZ/control answer pairs, own-family judges choose TRIZ **3.78 percentage points** more often than the other three judges (95% case bootstrap [1.38, 6.16], 45 cases). Both answers in each pair come from the same generator. This estimates a treatment-preference interaction, not own-output versus other-output self-preference.
