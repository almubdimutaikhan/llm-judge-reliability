# Limitations

The creative studies concern **one Mars-boot problem**. Sets share candidates and ranking comparisons share calls. More permutations and repeats characterize this problem more closely; they do not add independent tasks.

Lower removal diversity and greater session overlap measure consistency. The creative concepts have no gold quality ranking, so these outcomes do not establish better selection. Four Claude rankings, two ten-permutation outputs, and one session are incomplete; the analysis records their actual denominators and excludes the incomplete session.

The family diagnostic compares TRIZ and control answers from the same generator. It cannot identify own-output versus other-output self-preference. Its case-bootstrap interval applies to the treatment interaction in this selected 45-case casebase. Model-specific evaluation criteria, style, and familiarity remain possible explanations.

Temperature zero does not eliminate hosted-model variation. Logged model IDs are preserved, while provider routing and model revisions cannot be independently reconstructed. In particular, `anthropic/claude-sonnet-5` is an identifier in the logs, not a certified vendor version.

The released metadata supports offline analysis of parsed decisions. Generated concepts, raw model replies, and original prompts are outside this release. Alternative parsing and new inference require the source experiments. Usage fields are retained where recorded; this project makes no aggregate dollar-cost claim.
