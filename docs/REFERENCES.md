# References and credit

- **Lianmin Zheng et al. (2023).** [Judging LLM-as-a-Judge with MT-Bench and Chatbot Arena](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.05685). Biases in model-based evaluation.
- **Koki Wataoka, Tsubasa Takahashi, Ryokan Ri (2024; revised 2025).** [Self-Preference Bias in LLM-as-a-Judge](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.21819). Self-preference measurement and familiarity as an explanation for preference patterns.
- **Zhi-Yuan Chen et al. (2025).** [Beyond the Surface: Measuring Self-Preference in LLM Judgments](https://aclanthology.org/2025.emnlp-main.86/). Separating judge preference from answer quality with gold judgements.

Experiment sources: Almubdi Mutaikhan’s [BVSR project](https://github.com/almubdimutaikhan/bvrs-llm-creativity) and [TRIZ-2AFC framework](https://github.com/almubdimutaikhan/triz-2afc-agent-framework). This repository provides metadata exports and reproducible analyses of those experiments.

The related [TRIZ prompting study](https://github.com/almubdimutaikhan/triz-llm-evaluation) examines treatment effects. It shares jury records with the family analysis here; the two analyses are not independent replications. Source and export fingerprints are recorded in [the manifest](../data/manifest.json).
